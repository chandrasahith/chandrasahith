About

I got into Computer Science because I've always been curious about computers and wanted to understand how the things I use are actually built. What I enjoy most is building websites, especially the frontend — playing with UI, UX, and animations to make something feel good to use.

I also enjoy working on the backend because there's something interesting about understanding what happens behind what you see on the screen. I enjoy working across the stack and understanding how different parts of a system come together, from the user-facing interface to the services and APIs behind it. I'm particularly interested in writing clean, maintainable code and improving systems as they grow.

I'm still exploring different areas of technology and trying to get a little better at whatever I work on. Right now, I'm working on Optima, a project that helps people find what they're looking for at the best possible price. I'm currently a final-year Computer Science student, and most of my learning happens through projects, experimentation, and trying things for myself.
